# Castle Survivor — UX / UI Review and Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Build:** 58 (`c7b88e3`) · **Evidence:** `tools/shots/` captures at 1280×800, `index.html` CSS lines 9–1380 and markup 1382–1651, `PLAN.md` phases 17, 18.7, 21, 24, 27, `tools/reports/press-review-2026-09-26.md`
**Reference points:** Vampire Survivors (HUD restraint, pick-screen clarity), DRG: Survivor (end-of-run breakdown, weapon cards), Hades (typography discipline, voice), Diablo IV (co-op nameplates, boss bars)
**Mockups:** `tools/mockups/hud-solo.html`, `hud-coop.html`, `title.html`, `levelup.html`, `gameover.html` (1280×800 stages, open in a browser and screenshot next to the captures)

---

## 1. Executive summary

**What works.** The panel language is consistent: one dark-bronze gradient, one border colour, one gold. That discipline is rare in a hobby project and it should be kept. The top-centre `.hud-strip` column (index.html:904–911, PLAN 17.3) was the right call: nothing is drawn over the knight. The level-up card anatomy (icon, title, rarity tag, flavour line, delta line, rank counter — index.html:9159–9167) is already better than most shipped survivors-likes. Co-op rows are named, tinted and have real down/reviving states (index.html:1170–1194, PLAN 24). The end-of-run damage breakdown exists and found a real balance bug. The character-select portraits are lit (`title.png`). The event banners are tinted per event (index.html:1970–1974) and the revive banner says the mechanic in plain words. The bones are good.

**The three biggest problems.**

1. **The HUD does not know what matters.** Combo and streak (two pills, Cinzel 14px, top-centre, index.html:688–692 and 935–940) sit in the single most-looked-at spot on the screen in every in-play capture, while the boss bar is 12px tall and 360px wide (index.html:676) and the wave-event banner, which changes the rules of the fight, is a 16px one-liner *below* the boss bar (index.html:947). In `rev-mirefen-2.png` the stack top-to-bottom is COMBO → Haste pill → boss name → boss bar → BLESSED WIND: the least important thing is on top and the most important is fourth. The player's own HP bar has no numerals inside it; the number is a separate 12px MedievalSharp line (index.html:436) that reads "1S1 / 1S1" (`rev-kingsfield-1.png`, `coop-down.png`).
2. **Type is used at sizes it cannot survive.** Cinzel Decorative, a display face with swashes, is set at 20px for the wave counter (index.html:475) and 15px for the boss name (index.html:670): "MORGHUL THE DREAD" and "GNARLTUSK THE DEFILER" in `rev-mirefen-4.png` and `rev-emberreach-3.png` are gold mush. MedievalSharp, a hand-lettered face, is set at 11–12px in eleven places (xp-text 461, dash-label 724, wave-timer 834, menu-hint 1137, forge-item-rank 1098, help-item-stats 357, upgrade-delta 584, wave-event-desc 951, feats 1028, char-title 158, map-desc 195) and often in `#6d5637` or `#8a7040`, which is 2.9:1 and 4.0:1 against the panel. `menu-levelup-late.png`'s "2/5" rank tag (index.html:583, 11px, `#6d5637`) is the single most decision-relevant number on that screen and it is the least legible thing on it.
3. **The pre-run screen is one column trying to hold five decisions.** `title.png` and `menu-menu-after-run.png` stack title, three cards, bio, five battlefields, description, Ogre Level, companions, gold and six menu buttons into 800px, and the pinned BEGIN QUEST (index.html:1251–1257, the `max-height: 1000px` rule) covers the Ogre Level ladder. The compaction media query at index.html:1261 is not the culprit at 1280×800; the pinned button from the *broader* rule is. A nine-year-old sees a wall; a parent on a laptop cannot find the difficulty setting at all.

**Design direction, in one paragraph.** Keep the bronze-and-gold panel language, drop the parchment-tinted body copy in favour of a single warm off-white, and re-cut the HUD around one rule: *the eye goes top-centre for threats, top-left for "am I alive", top-right for "how far", bottom for "what I have".* Boss and event own the top-centre; combo and streak are demoted to a small counter in the right panel. Every knight gets the same row, in their colour, with numerals inside the bar in Cinzel, and every knight gets a nameplate in the world in co-op. Cinzel Decorative is for screen titles at 32px and up, nothing else; Cinzel carries every label and every number; MedievalSharp carries flavour text at 14px and up. Level-up cards get four visibly different silhouettes (stat, weapon, evolution, relic) and stat cards learn to say which evolution they feed. The pre-run screen becomes two steps: a title with one button, and a two-column "muster" screen where the Ogre Level can never be covered.

### 2.2 Typography

The three-font system is a good idea executed at the wrong sizes.

| Face | Where it earns its place | Where it fights |
|---|---|---|
| **Cinzel Decorative 900** | Screen titles: CASTLE SURVIVOR (52px), LEVEL UP (46px), VICTORY / FALLEN (64px), PAUSED (42px). At those sizes the swashes are the game's signature. | Wave counter at 20px (index.html:475), boss name at 15px (670), grade letter is fine at 72px. Below ~28px the counters fill in and the caps read as a blur, worst on the "M" and "R" (`rev-mirefen-4.png`). Codex/Forge/Hall titles at 36px are borderline but acceptable. |
| **Cinzel 700** | Labels, names, buttons, all numerals. It has a clean 1/5/7 and even widths. The card titles at 18px (539) are the best type on any screen. | Set at 10–11px in eight places (weapon-slot-level 876, char-owner 136, scores th 1004, forge fm-title 1106, coop-wpn b 1166, build-badge 30). At 10px with `letter-spacing: 2px` the small caps lose their serifs. Minimum is 12px, ideally 13. |
| **MedievalSharp** | Flavour: card descriptions ("Thy blade strikes truer"), event descriptions, bios, loading quotes. It carries the "storybook read aloud to a kid" voice and it should stay. | **Numbers** (health-text 436, xp-text 460, coop-hp-text 1185, pause-stats 604, game-over-stats 647, scores-table 1001, dmg-breakdown 1367). Its numerals are irregular by design; "1S1" and "S75 XP" are the result. Also at 11–12px for hints (724, 1137, 1223, 1229, 1231): the x-height collapses and `#6d5637` on `#0c0602` is 2.9:1. |

**Sizes at 1280×800, measured against the captures.** Card body copy 14px MedievalSharp `#8a7040` (index.html:542) is 4.0:1 and hairline; it wants 15px and `#c9b48a`-class. HUD stat lines 13px Cinzel `#c9a253` (470–472) are fine. Menu hints 11px `#6d5637` (1137) fail at any size. Codex item stats 12px `#7a9a5a` (356–358) pass contrast at 6:1 but are too small for a body face. Settings labels 14px Cinzel are correct.

**On a phone** (`mobile-ingame.png`): the left panel at 0.85 scale puts the HP numerals at ~10px MedievalSharp. Unreadable. Numerals need to be *inside* the bar in Cinzel so that the bar height (18px) is the type's floor.

**Voice.** "Thee/thy" works in card flavour ("Blessed shields circle thee"), event descriptions ("The wind carries thee swiftly"), and the loading quotes, because those are the game speaking. It breaks in system UI: "Time Endured", "Foes Vanquished", "Renown", "Glory" (index.html:9862–9873, `menu-gameover-brennan.png`) are archaic *labels* and a nine-year-old has to decode them; "Glory: 13060" in the HUD (index.html:9370) is the score and should say so or be dropped. The rule: **flavour text may be archaic; labels, numbers and instructions are plain.** The revive banner (PLAN 24.3: "RUN TO THEM AND STAND CLOSE — NO BUTTON NEEDED") is the model.

### 2.3 Art direction and style

- **Three visual registers with no bridge.** (1) The title plates are painted, saturated, high-contrast fantasy illustration (`castleBackgroundTitle.png`, `castleSurvivorTitleScreen.png`). (2) The panels are flat dark-bronze with a hairline `#5a3a1a` border and a gold gradient bar. (3) The 3D scene is stylised low-poly with bloom. The panel language is closest to Diablo's and is the right anchor, but it borrows nothing from the painting: no lion crest, no banner shape, no filigree corner. One repeated motif (the lion from the shield on the art, as a 24px corner mark on panels and as the P1 badge) would tie 1 and 2 together for near-zero cost. Do **not** add texture to the panels; the transparency over five different grounds is what keeps them legible.
- **The icon set is the strongest asset in the UI.** `images/upgrades/*.png` (Codex `menu-codex.png`, Forge `menu-forge.png`, cards `menu-levelup.png`) are consistent, painterly, well-lit and read at 48px. They deserve larger use: 64px on cards, 24px with names in the end-of-run breakdown, 32px in the weapon HUD. Relics use emoji (index.html:8380–8388 `icon: '🪶'`); at 14px inside a 30px pill (577–580) they render as the OS's emoji font and are the one thing on screen in a different art style. The `relic-pill-img` path (581) exists, so the fix is to generate the nine relic icons through `gen-icons.mjs` and never draw emoji in the HUD.
- **Enemy readability is a UI problem as well as a lighting one.** The press review's fix (delete `colorMul: 0.55`) is right, but even fully lit, 20px goblins on a 1280px frame need a *UI* layer: the overhead HP bar (index.html:814–824, 50×5px, red on black) only appears on damage, and there is no ground marker for archers. The lowest-cost UI answer is a 2px ring in the enemy's threat colour (archer amber, shaman violet, bomber orange) drawn as a decal, which 21.1 removed because the tinted *models* washed out. Rings are not tints. Restore the ring, keep the model untouched.
- **The player ring** at low alpha is the only thing anchoring the knight in `rev-mirefen-4.png` and `rev-kingsfield-4.png`. In co-op it must be per-knight and paired with a nameplate. The ring itself is fine; it needs to be a solid 3px line in the knight's tint at 0.9 alpha with a soft 12px glow, not a 40px soft blob.
- **Colour tokens are duplicated, not named.** `#c9a253` appears 60+ times, `#e8c860` 40+, `#8a7040` 30+, `#5a3a1a` 40+. There is one `:root` variable (`--title-bg`, index.html:12). Section 3.7 gives a token table; lifting it into `:root` is a mechanical find-and-replace and makes every later change one line.

### 2.4 Screen flow

```
loading → title(+char+map+ogre+coop+gold+6 buttons) → run → level-up ×~46 → end → [Rise Again | Hall | Menu]
                                          ↳ Forge / Hall / Settings / Help(Codex) / Level Editor / Load Map
```

- **Everything is on one screen and nothing is one click deep.** That is the wrong trade at 800px tall. Character, battlefield and Ogre Level are decisions with pictures; Forge/Hall/Codex/Settings are destinations. The six-button `.menu-row` (index.html:1451–1459) gives LEVEL EDITOR and LOAD MAP, which are dev tools, the same weight as THE FORGE. A first-time nine-year-old sees: a title, three portraits, five green rectangles, a hidden row, and BEGIN QUEST. They will press BEGIN QUEST, which is fine, but they will never find Ogre Level 1 without a parent.
- **The title screen has no "what happened last time".** `menu-menu-after-run.png` is identical to `title.png` bar the P1 badge moving. Hades, DRG:S and VS all put the last result and the next unlock on the first screen. The Hall of Fame (`menu-scores.png`) is empty with 13 feats listed in 12px MedievalSharp `#6d5637`: the feat system exists and is invisible.
- **Level-up is well-structured but flat at depth.** `menu-levelup-late.png` (wave 15: three 1/5–2/5 stat cards with 3 rerolls in hand) is what the press review flagged. The UI can help even before the pool grows: show which evolution each stat feeds (`WEAPON_EVOLUTIONS[*].requires`, index.html:8874–8886, is already the data), show rank as pips, show the delta as a bar, and make REROLL the visual peer of the cards when the player has charges and every card is a stat.
- **End of run** (`menu-gameover-brennan.png`): four unlock lines at the top in 13px blue, then "Time Endured", then a 13-row two-column list in 20px MedievalSharp, then gold earned, then the personal best, then (off-screen at 800px) the damage breakdown and the three buttons. The most interesting information — the build and where the damage came from — is below the fold, and the most important number for a kid — gold — is 3/4 of the way down. The grade letter (72px Cinzel Decorative, index.html:956–960) is the right idea and should be the anchor.
- **Codex** (`menu-codex.png`, `menu-codex-tab3.png`): good structure, blank canvases on cold open (press review confirms model parse after open, index.html:12485–12515). Needs a silhouette placeholder and the RETURN button also at the bottom of a scrolled list.
- **Forge** (`menu-forge.png`): the "who" tabs (index.html:1047–1059) are clipped off the top at 800px; the gold total is the headline (correct); the mastery list at the bottom is 12px MedievalSharp and scrolls off. Fine as a screen, wrong vertical budget.
- **Settings** (`menu-settings.png`): correct. Lighting sliders are dev controls and should collapse under an "Advanced" disclosure next to Level Editor and Load Map.
- **Pause** (`menu-pause.png`): 17 rows of 17px MedievalSharp. Pause should show the build (weapons with levels, relics) as icons plus five stats, not a stat dump. The `coop-kit` row is the right component.

## 5. What was already tried, and why this does not repeat it

- PLAN 17.3 centralised transients into the `.hud-strip` column. This spec keeps the column and changes the *order and weight* inside it; it does not put anything back over the knight.
- PLAN 21.1 removed enemy recolours because tinted models washed out. The rings proposed in 2.3 are ground decals, not material tints, and the models keep their own materials.
- PLAN 24.1 gave companions named rows. This spec makes P1's row the *same* component so there is one grammar, and adds the in-world nameplate that 24 did not.
- PLAN 18.7 compacted the title for phones and keyed it on width. The Ogre Level bug is the broader `max-height: 1000px` pin, not the phone rule; the fix is structural (footer inside the flow), so both phone and 800px desktop are covered by one layout.
- PLAN 27 made picks per-knight; the level-up spec keeps the owner line and tint frame from 24.2/27.2 and changes only the card anatomy.
