# Castle Survivor Critic Report -- 2026-09-20

## Verdict

Castle Survivor has gone from a broken prototype to a playable survivors-style game in a remarkably compressed timeline. The core loop works: weapons level, builds diverge at the 4-slot limit, chests gate evolutions correctly, and the stand-still bot dies at wave 4 as intended. The title screen, character select, Forge, Hall of Fame, controller support, and Codex are all present and functional. What holds the build back from the Vampire Survivors / DRG:S bar is shallowness -- three characters with near-identical progression, only 2 of 10 weapons locked, no passive item layer, and Parker dying 3 waves before the other two knights. The single 8,473-line file, a per-run texture leak, and procedural-only audio further cap the ceiling. This is a strong alpha that needs depth, differentiation, and polish more than new systems.

**Overall: 5.7 / 10**

## Top 10 Recommendations

### 1. Fix Parker's DPS deficit (S)

**What:** Parker dies at wave 7 while Dad/Brennan reach wave 10.

**Why:** Quickblade L1 deals 12 damage at 0.7s = 17.1 DPS, which looks higher than sword's 12.5 DPS. But cleave[1]=1 means he only full-damages 2 targets per swing, while Dad's arc hits wider. Parker's evolution multiplier is 1.5x vs 2.0x for sword/spear. In the kite log Parker took 251 HP from archer arrows by wave 7 -- his low range (3.0 units) forces him closer to melee packs, spending more time in archer sightlines without the armor or HP to absorb it.

**How:** In `WEAPON_STATS.quickblade`: raise base damage from [12,17,23,30,40] to [14,19,26,34,45], widen cleave from [1,1,2,2,3] to [1,2,2,3,4], and set evo dmgMul from 1.5 to 1.7. In `CHARACTER_PASSIVES.parker.apply`: add +1 armor so he survives archer chip. Target: Parker kite bot reaches wave 9-10.

### 2. Reduce archer arrow dominance (S)

**What:** Archer arrows are the #1 damage source for all three characters in the kite log -- 276 (Dad), 408 (Brennan), 251 (Parker). No other source comes close.

**Why:** Archers spawn from wave 4 with `damage: 4 + wave * 0.8`, keeping 8-14 range while strafing. By wave 10, each arrow deals ~12 raw damage. With the 2.6s cooldown and 4+ archers from the archerLine formation, the player takes a steady drip of undodgeable damage from off-screen.

**How:** In `spawnEnemy` (index.html:2812-2815): reduce archer damage scaling from `4 + state.wave * 0.8` to `3 + state.wave * 0.5`. Increase `attackCooldown` from 2.6 to 3.0. Alternatively, make archers stop to fire (increase wind-up from their current implicit 0) so the player gets a visual cue. Target: archer damage drops to 2nd or 3rd damage source behind melee ogres.

### 3. Add a passive item / accessory layer (M)

**What:** All build decisions are weapon choices. There is no passive item category.

**Why:** This is the single biggest depth gap versus VS/DRG:S. VS has ~30 passive items (Empty Tome, Spinach, Duplicator, etc.) that combine with weapons to create builds. Castle Survivor's stat upgrades (Whetstone, Boots, Chainmail) are simple number boosts with no interaction effects. Adding 6-8 passive items with conditional effects would double build diversity.

**How:** Add a `PASSIVE_ITEMS` array alongside `UPGRADES`. Candidates: "Glass Cannon" (+20% damage, -15 maxHP), "Thorn Mail" (reflect 5 damage on hit, requires 3+ armor), "Arcane Focus" (crits reduce weapon cooldowns by 0.2s), "Blood Pact" (lifesteal 2 HP/kill but -1 regen), "Magnet Boots" (pickup range +4, speed +0.5). Gate them behind weapon or stat thresholds. Max 3 passive slots. Each item shows "before/after" on the level-up card. Size: ~200-300 lines in the upgrade pool logic.

### 4. Lock more weapons behind achievements (S)

**What:** Only 2 of 10 weapons are locked (Arrow Volley: wave 5, Arcane Bomb: boss kill). A new player sees 8/10 weapons on their first run.

**Why:** Discovery and progressive unlock are the meta-loop engine for this genre. VS gates most weapons behind character or challenge unlocks. When everything is available from run 1, there is no "I wonder what that weapon does" motivation to keep playing.

**How:** Expand `WEAPON_UNLOCKS` (index.html:6054):
- `wardShields`: "Win a run on Ogre Level 0" (teaches defense)
- `stormCall`: "Kill 50 enemies in a single combo" (teaches combos)
- `emberTrail`: "Play as Parker and reach wave 10" (cross-character discovery)
- `holyAura`: "Reach wave 10 with Dad" (encourages longer runs)

Keep sword/spear/quickblade (primaries) and dagger always available.

### 5. Add off-screen threat indicators for archers (S)

**What:** Archers keep 8-14 range and strafe. They fire from off-screen and the player has no warning until the arrow hits.

**Why:** The `showDamageDirection` system exists but fires reactively (after a hit). A proactive indicator -- a small red arc on the screen edge when an archer enters firing range -- would let players dodge and would make archer damage feel fair rather than ambient chip.

**How:** In `updateEnemies`, when an archer sets `attackTimer` to fire, add a brief directional arrow indicator on the HUD edge pointing toward the archer (reuse the swarm indicator pattern at `_swarmIndicatorAngle`). Give it 0.4s lead time so the player can dash. This also makes Warding Shields (swats arrows) feel more valuable when the player can see what it is blocking.

### 6. Differentiate enemy visuals at distance (M)

**What:** Elites, speed goblins, and regular goblins use the same model with only emissive tint differences (0x2288ff for speed, slightly larger scale for elites). Archers reuse the goblin rig with a bow.

**Why:** At the camera distance shown in run-dad.png and run-brennan.png, the models are 30-40 pixels tall. An emissive tint is invisible at that scale. Players cannot prioritize threats they cannot identify.

**How:** For elites: add a visible particle aura (the `eliteAura` VFX function exists in vfx.js:4219 and accounts for 0.9% of sim time). For archers: tint their whole model distinctly (a brighter yellow-green) and attach a visible quiver mesh, or scale them differently (0.9x to read as "ranged" vs 1.0x melee). For speed goblins: add a blur/streak trail. This is a visual communication problem, not an art problem.

### 7. Fix the texture/geometry leak (S)

**What:** 34 textures and 30 geometries are not freed on returnToMenu (38 baseline to 133 at menu, profile-dad-kingsfield.log:41-44).

**Why:** Each run clones enemy materials (`c.material = c.material.clone()` at index.html:2873). `disposeEnemy` presumably calls `material.dispose()`, but if the cloned material's `.map` (texture) is shared with the original, disposing the material does not dispose the texture. After 3-4 runs on mobile, the VRAM will cause stuttering or a crash.

**How:** In `disposeEnemy`, after `mat.dispose()`, call `mat.map?.dispose()` for each cloned material. Verify by running `textureReport()` via the debug hook before and after. Also check that `_hpBarPool` sprites dispose their canvas textures (index.html:7046 does `map?.dispose()` -- verify that path runs for all bars). Target: baseline textures (38) restored within +2 after returnToMenu.

### 8. Record 5 key SFX assets (M -- external dependency)

**What:** All audio is procedural noise+tone patches (index.html:2078-2116). No recorded sound effects exist.

**Why:** Procedural audio is technically impressive but perceptually flat. A sword hit sounds like filtered white noise because it is. Compare VS where every weapon has a distinct, layered sound. Even 5 recordings would transform feel:
1. Sword impact (metal + flesh thud)
2. Enemy death pop (satisfying squish)
3. Level-up jingle (harp arpeggio)
4. Boss roar (on spawn)
5. Coin pickup (glass clink)

**How:** `AudioSystem.loadAll` already expects filenames for recorded assets (PLAN 6.3). Record or source 5 WAV files, convert to MP3, drop into `audio/`, and the system will prefer them over the procedural fallback. The low-pass muffle and volume controls already work for loaded sounds.

### 9. Add character unlock progression (S)

**What:** All 3 characters are available from run 1. There is no reason to replay as a different knight beyond trying their weapon.

**Why:** In VS, unlocking a new character is the primary reward loop for the first 10 hours. It contextualizes early runs as "earning something" even when you die at wave 5. Castle Survivor's characters have meaningfully different playstyles (sword sweep vs spear thrust vs quickblade dash) but the player has no reason to discover that progression.

**How:** Lock Brennan behind "Reach wave 8 with Dad" and Parker behind "Win a run with any character". Show locked cards with a silhouette and the unlock condition. This costs ~20 lines in character select rendering and a `meta.unlockedCharacters` check. It immediately creates a 3-run minimum discovery arc.

### 10. Split the monolith (L)

**What:** 8,473 lines of game logic, rendering, UI, audio, and data in one `index.html` file.

**Why:** Every change requires navigating a ~7,000-line script block. The harness only validates via smoke/balance bots -- there are no unit tests. The 15 JS errors in the kite balance log are invisible without the harness. Extracting data and systems into modules would make each system independently testable and reviewable.

**How:** Extract in this order (each step is independently deployable):
1. `data.js`: WEAPON_STATS, OGRE_LEVELS, UPGRADES, FORGE_UPGRADES, WAVE_SCRIPT, FORMATIONS, MAPS, enemy stat tables (~800 lines)
2. `audio.js`: AudioSystem IIFE (~300 lines, already self-contained)
3. `menu.js`: menu navigation, settings, Forge, Codex, Hall of Fame (~1,200 lines)
4. `game.js`: updateGame and everything it calls (~3,500 lines)
5. `main.js`: init, asset loading, Three.js setup (~1,000 lines)

Use `<script type="module">` and `import/export`. The data tables have zero cross-dependencies; audio is already an IIFE. Start with step 1 -- it is zero-risk and makes balance tuning a one-file review.

## Bugs and Risks

1. **Texture/geometry leak per run** (index.html:2873 `material.clone()` + clearRun disposal): 34 textures and 30 geometries accumulate per run cycle. Will cause VRAM exhaustion on mobile after 4-5 runs. See Recommendation 7.

2. **15 JS errors in kite balance log** (balance-kite-kingsfield.log:40): The log says "JS ERRORS (15):" but lists no details. These errors are silent to the player but indicate runtime exceptions that may affect game state. Need to check the playtest harness error capture -- it may be truncating the error list.

3. **Wave 7 sim measurement anomaly** (profile-dad-kingsfield.log:9): `sim avg 0.00ms` with 132 enemies. This is impossible -- likely a profiler measurement bug where the sampling window missed all sim ticks. The game was probably paused (level-up screen) during that wave's sample window.

4. **Parker's `_dashCDBonus` is set but not validated** (index.html:1371): `state.player._dashCDBonus = 0.5` is set in the passive but `getDashCooldown` subtracts it from 2.5. If a Forge upgrade or rarity multiplier stacks additional cooldown reduction, the dash cooldown could go negative. Add `Math.max(0.5, ...)` in `getDashCooldown`.

5. **`clearRun` resets `banishCharges` to 1** (index.html:7114): This gives a free banish every run regardless of Forge rank. Compare `rerollCharges = 0` on the line above -- rerolls start at 0 and are only added by Forge. Banish should also start at 0 + Forge ranks. Currently every player gets 1 free banish, which is likely intentional but inconsistent with rerolls.

6. **`disposeEnemy` is called but not shown in the read window**: Verify it exists and disposes all cloned materials, textures, and the mixer. A missing `mixer.stopAllAction()` before disposal can leave orphaned animation clips in the Three.js animation cache.

7. **`burnPatches.length = 0` in clearRun** (index.html:7126): Burn patches are cleared by zeroing the array, but their Three.js meshes are not removed from the scene or disposed. Each patch likely has a PlaneGeometry + MeshBasicMaterial. Over multiple runs this leaks scene children.

8. **Charge telegraph geometry/material lifecycle** (index.html:3350-3357): `_chargeGeo` is reused (good), but `new THREE.MeshBasicMaterial(...)` is created per charge and only disposed in `endPattern`. If the boss dies during a charge telegraph, `endPattern` is called, but verify the path through `killEnemy` -> does it call `endPattern` for an active pattern? If not, the telegraph group and material leak.

9. **Brennan kite bot profiler log timed out** (profile-brennan-kingsfield.log): `TimeoutError: Waiting failed: 180000ms exceeded`. The Brennan profiler run failed entirely. Only the Dad profiler log is usable.

## What I Could Not Assess

- **Darkwood map balance**: No bot logs exist for Darkwood. The wolf x2.0 / bat x1.7 / rat x0.5 weight skew (index.html:2357) and 230 dense pines suggest kiting room is severely constrained, but without a run log the difficulty cannot be quantified.
- **Real-device frame rate**: The profiler runs in headless Chrome with software WebGL. Real GPU performance with bloom + shadows on a mid-range laptop or Steam Deck is unknown. The adaptive quality system exists but has not been validated against a real framerate target.
- **Mobile / touch input**: No touch controls exist. The game requires keyboard or controller.
- **Late-game balance (waves 15-20)**: No bot has survived past wave 10. The late-game difficulty curve (HP * (1 + (wave-10) * 0.10), damage * (1 + (wave-10) * 0.06)) and the final boss encounter are untested by any automated run.
- **Multiplayer / content longevity**: How many runs before a player has maxed the Forge, seen all evolutions, and exhausted discovery. With 3 characters, 2 maps, and 6 Ogre Levels, the combinatorial space is small.
- **Audio on real hardware**: Procedural SFX are generated per-play through Web Audio. Latency, clipping, and polyphony under load have not been measured.
