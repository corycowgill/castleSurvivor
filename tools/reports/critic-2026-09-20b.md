# Castle Survivor Critic Report -- 2026-09-20b (Second Full Review)

## Verdict

Castle Survivor has moved from a playable alpha to a credible indie survivors game in the two weeks since the first review. The relic layer, splat-painted terrain, per-map lighting, feathered streams, priority-target ground rings, archer off-screen warnings, shaman as a support enemy, Ogre King guard phases, and walk-through clutter represent a broad push across depth, legibility, feel, and map quality. The single biggest depth gap from the first review (no passive item layer) is closed: 9 relics with conditional interactions, 3 slots, offered alongside weapons and stats at level-up. The look pass took the scene from programmer-art green plane to a warm medieval-fantasy level with distinct Kingsfield/Darkwood moods. The bot data shows all three knights winning Ogre 0 on both maps, the stand-still bot dying at wave 4, and Ogre 2 providing real challenge (waves 6-11 deaths).

What holds the build back: a texture/geometry leak that worsened (47 textures per run cycle, up from 34), procedural-only audio (no recorded SFX or music beyond the battle loop), Brennan's 40% death rate on Kingsfield Ogre 0 (4 wins out of 10), Darkwood still too dark in areas away from lanterns, and the monolith growing to 9,243 lines. The discovery drip remains shallow: 6 of 10 weapons and all 9 relics are available from run 1.

**Overall: 6.6 / 10** (was 5.7)

## New Top 10 Recommendations

### 1. Fix the texture and geometry leak (S)

**What:** 47 textures and 17 geometries accumulate per run cycle and are never freed.

**Why:** This is the same bug from the first review (#7) and it got worse. Profile-gpu-look.log shows: baseline 96 textures, 199 after first run's returnToMenu, 246 after second run's returnToMenu. The growth is +47 textures per run. At this rate, a 4-run session on a device with 2GB VRAM will exhaust it. The sim performance is excellent (0.10ms avg), but the leak will cause frame drops and crashes on mobile or integrated GPUs before the player ever sees wave 10 of their fourth run.

**Root cause:** `disposeEnemy` (line ~1497) calls `_materials[i].dispose()` but never calls `.map?.dispose()` on the cloned material's textures. When an enemy is spawned (line ~3347), its materials are cloned (`c.material = c.material.clone()`), creating a new material that shares the source model's `.map` texture. Disposing the material leaves the texture reference alive. Additionally, `_texCache` at line ~2484 keeps all loaded textures alive permanently (never cleared on returnToMenu). Canvas textures for HP bars, damage numbers, and glow discs created during a run are not all reclaimed.

**How:** In `disposeEnemy`, after `mat.dispose()`, add:
```js
if (mat.map && mat.map !== mat._srcMap) mat.map.dispose();
if (mat.normalMap) mat.normalMap.dispose();
if (mat.emissiveMap) mat.emissiveMap.dispose();
```
Mark the source model's textures so they are not disposed (they are shared). In `clearRun`, dispose orphaned canvas textures (check `renderer.info.memory.textures` before and after). Add a `textureReport()` call to the harness leak check that lists texture kinds.

**Target:** After returnToMenu, textures within +5 of baseline (96). After second run, within +5 of first run. Verify with `node tools/playtest.mjs profile`.

**Impact/hour:** HIGH. Known code path, ~2-3h.

### 2. Stabilise Brennan's early-game variance (S)

**What:** Brennan dies before wave 8 in 6 of 10 Kingsfield runs at Ogre 0 (deaths at waves 5, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11; 4 wins). His worst runs end at wave 5 with only ~100 kills.

**Why:** Brennan has 100 base HP (vs Dad's 115), no armor passive, and a melee-range spear. His wave-5 deaths correlate with not drawing a weapon or stat upgrade in the first 6 levels -- when he enters the archer wave (wave 4) with only L1 spear, he lacks the DPS to clear archers before they fire 3-4 volleys. The 2.2x owned-weapon weighting (PLAN 12.13) helps but does not eliminate runs where the first 4 level-ups are all non-weapon stats. Archers remain his #1 damage source in every recorded run (357 HP in the Darkwood death, 96 in the Kingsfield-2 win, 314 in the earlier Kingsfield win).

**How:** Two changes:
1. In `CHARACTER_PASSIVES.brennan.apply`: add `state.player.maxHP += 10` (110 base, still below Dad's 115). This gives Brennan one more archer hit of margin in the early game.
2. Guarantee at least one weapon upgrade in the first 3 level-ups: in `generateUpgrade`, if `state.player.level <= 3` and no weapon upgrade has been offered in this run, force one weapon card into the trio. This prevents the "4 stat-only level-ups" scenario that causes the wave-5 deaths.

**Target:** Brennan's Kingsfield Ogre 0 win rate rises from 40% to 60%+ over 10 bot runs. Wave-5 deaths drop to 0-1.

**Impact/hour:** HIGH. ~1-2h. Two small code changes with measurable bot verification.

### 3. Gate relics behind feats (S)

**What:** All 9 relics are available from run 1. A new player can see the entire relic pool in their first 3 runs.

**Why:** The relic layer is the game's biggest new depth addition, but it has zero discovery gating. VS gates passive items behind character unlocks and achievement conditions. The current setup means a player who plays 5 runs has already been offered every relic. Compare: the weapon unlock system (4 locked behind feats) creates a "what does that do?" pull. Relics should do the same.

**How:** In `RELICS`, add an `unlock` field (same pattern as `WEAPON_UNLOCKS`):
- Phoenix Feather: always available (the safety relic for new players)
- Wolfsbane Charm: always available (teaches crowd mechanics)
- Greed Idol: always available (simple and obvious)
- Hunter's Mark: "Defeat 500 enemies in a single run" (mid-tier challenge)
- Berserker's Oath: "Win a run below 30% HP at victory" (risk-reward discovery)
- Warding Runes: "Take 200 damage from archers across runs" (teaches the archer problem)
- Windwalker Boots: "Dash 100 times across runs" (teaches dash as a mechanic)
- Bloodletter: "Land 50 critical hits in a single run" (teaches crit builds)
- Thundering Hooves: "Win with Parker" (cross-character discovery)

Show locked relics in the Codex with a silhouette and unlock condition. This creates a 10-15 run discovery arc for relics.

**Target:** 3 relics available from run 1, 6 locked behind feats. Average player unlocks all by run 15.

**Impact/hour:** HIGH. ~2h. Data changes + Codex rendering + meta storage.

### 4. Record 5 key SFX (M -- external dependency)

**What:** All audio is still procedural noise+tone patches. No recorded sound effects exist.

**Why:** This is unchanged from the first review's #8. The look pass dramatically improved visual feel (3/10 to 6.5/10), but audio is now the single largest gap between Castle Survivor and the reference games. A sword swing sounds like filtered white noise because it is. The game's visual quality now raises expectations that the audio cannot meet. Five recordings would close the gap:

1. **Sword/weapon impact** (metal + flesh thud, 0.2s)
2. **Enemy death pop** (satisfying squish/burst, 0.15s)
3. **Level-up chime** (harp arpeggio or crystal ring, 0.5s)
4. **Boss roar** (on spawn, guttural snarl, 0.8s)
5. **Coin pickup** (glass clink, 0.1s)

**How:** `AudioSystem.loadAll` already expects filenames for recorded assets. Record or source 5 WAV files (freesound.org CC0 or generated via Stable Audio), convert to MP3, drop into `audio/`. The existing low-pass muffle, volume controls, and polyphony system will work with loaded sounds.

**Impact/hour:** HIGH per asset. ~1h integration + sourcing time (external).

### 5. Fine-tune Darkwood fill light (S)

**What:** Props and the knight are near-invisible in Darkwood areas away from lanterns. The farmstead and ford are too dark despite a first-pass fill-light raise (PLAN 15.2c).

**Why:** The critic-look review flagged this at 6.5/10 and it was partly addressed (exposure raised to 1.08, hemi adjusted). But the gameplay screenshots (gpu-darkwood-2.png farmstead, gpu-darkwood-4.png ford) still show the knight merging into the ground. The hero glow ring helps (fix2-darkwood-1.png) but cannot compensate for props that are silhouettes. D4's dark areas maintain material detail via fill lights and rim light on the hero.

**How:** In `MAPS.darkwood.look`:
- Raise `ambientIntensity` by 0.15 (one more step beyond the first raise).
- Add a subtle warm fill to `hemiGround` (shift from deep blue toward blue-green).
- Raise lantern `intensity` from 15 to 20 so the warm pools punch through.
- Consider a dedicated rim-light uniform on the hero's material in Darkwood (emissive 0.06 on MeshStandardMaterial, driven by `look.heroEmissive`).

**Target:** Props show material detail at zoom 1.5 on the farmstead shot without washing out the moonlit mood. Knight readable at the ford without the glow ring doing all the work.

**Impact/hour:** HIGH. ~30-45min. Constant tweaks + one re-capture.

### 6. Handle the map boundary (S)

**What:** The camera can expose black void at the edge of the map (visible in run-parker.png: right half of the screen is pure black).

**Why:** The world has a finite extent. When the player walks near the edge, the camera follows and shows the void beyond the ground plane. This is jarring and breaks immersion. VS handles this with an infinite-scrolling parallax background; D3 uses fog and off-screen geometry. Any visible boundary should read as "you can't go there" (a wall, fog, or a dark forest), not as "the world ends."

**How:** Three options, from cheapest to best:
1. **Camera clamp:** Clamp the camera target to keep the viewport inside the ground extent minus a margin. The player can still walk to the edge but the camera stops following. Cost: ~15 lines.
2. **Edge fog:** Ramp fog density up sharply in the outer 15% of the map so the boundary fades to the fog colour. Cost: ~30 lines (modify the fog uniform per frame based on camera distance to edge).
3. **Extended ground:** Extend the ground plane 50 units beyond the playable area with a darker/foggier variant. Cost: resize the ground mesh.

Option 1 is the quickest win. Options 2-3 are additive.

**Target:** No black void visible at any camera position during a full run.

**Impact/hour:** MEDIUM. ~1h for the camera clamp.

### 7. Smooth the Ogre Level difficulty curve (M)

**What:** Ogre 0 is reliably beatable (all knights win). Ogre 2 kills all three knights between waves 6-11 (balance-kite-kingsfield-ogre2.log). The gap between "normal" and "hard" is too steep.

**Why:** Ogre Level 1 is the missing step. The bot data only shows Ogre 0 (everyone wins) and Ogre 2 (everyone dies). If Ogre 1 is proportionally between them, a player who wins Ogre 0 should be able to reach Ogre 1 with a good build and survive to wave 12-15. Currently the jump from "comfortable win" to "wave 6 death" is abrupt. VS's difficulty scaling (Hyper -> Hurry Up) raises damage and speed gradually; DRG:S Hazard levels are 15-20% increments.

**How:** Verify Ogre 1 difficulty by running `node tools/playtest.mjs balance --ogre 1` for all three knights. If it is too close to Ogre 2 (deaths before wave 10), reduce the per-Ogre-Level HP/damage multiplier from its current values. Target: Ogre 1 should produce wins with strong builds and deaths at waves 12-15 with weak builds; Ogre 2 should produce deaths at waves 8-12 (currently 6-11); Ogre 3+ should be the "survival endurance" tier.

**Target:** Ogre 1 win rate 30-50% for all knights. Ogre 2 win rate ~10%.

**Impact/hour:** MEDIUM. ~2-3h (bot runs + tuning).

### 8. Raise quickblade evolution multiplier (S)

**What:** Quickblade's evolution (Tempest Edge) has `dmgMul: 1.5` while sword (Excalibur) and spear (Dragonlance) have `dmgMul: 2.0`.

**Why:** Parker compensates with faster attack speed (0.4s vs 0.75s sword, 0.95s spear) and the +1 armor passive. But in the late game (waves 15-20), evolution damage is a large fraction of total DPS. A 25% lower evolution multiplier means Parker's evolved damage falls behind Dad and Brennan when builds are full, even though his base DPS is competitive. The first review recommended raising it to 1.7; this was not done.

**How:** In `WEAPON_STATS.quickblade.evolution`: change `dmgMul` from `1.5` to `1.7`. Verify with a Parker Ogre 0 kite run that his kill count and clear time remain comparable to Dad and Brennan (currently 6,279 kills at 16:34 vs Dad's 6,402 at 16:36).

**Target:** Parker's evolved DPS within 10% of Dad's and Brennan's at wave 15+.

**Impact/hour:** MEDIUM. ~20min code + 30min bot verification.

### 9. Extend bot testing to Ogre Levels 1-3 and waves 15-20 (M)

**What:** No bot has completed a run above Ogre 0. Late-game balance (waves 15-20) at higher Ogre Levels is untested by any automated run.

**Why:** The Ogre Level system is the game's difficulty axis, but only the floor (Ogre 0) and a mid-tier (Ogre 2) have been tested. The HP/damage scaling past wave 10 (`HP * (1 + (wave-10) * 0.10)`, `damage * (1 + (wave-10) * 0.06)`) has never been validated against a strong evolved build at Ogre 1+. The Ogre King guard phases (66%/33% immune + summons) have been tested at Ogre 0 only. At Ogre 3+ the guard-phase summons could be unkillable if the ogre wall HP scales too fast.

**How:** Add a `--ogre N` flag to the balance harness (or verify it works). Run:
- All 3 knights at Ogre 1 on Kingsfield (3 runs)
- All 3 knights at Ogre 2 on Kingsfield (3 runs, extend existing data)
- Dad at Ogre 3 on Kingsfield (1 run, to see if the scaling breaks)

Log wave-of-death, damage breakdown, and whether guard-phase summons are cleared. This data will drive the Ogre Level tuning in recommendation #7.

**Target:** Bot data for Ogre 1-3, confirming the difficulty curve is smooth and no mechanic breaks at high scaling.

**Impact/hour:** MEDIUM. ~2-3h (mostly bot runtime + analysis).

### 10. Extract data tables to data.js (L, first step is S)

**What:** WEAPON_STATS, ENEMY_TYPES, OGRE_LEVELS, UPGRADES, FORGE_UPGRADES, WAVE_SCRIPT, FORMATIONS, RELICS, CHARACTER_PASSIVES, WEAPON_UNLOCKS, and WEAPON_EVOLUTIONS are defined inline in the 9,243-line index.html.

**Why:** These tables are the most-edited part of the codebase (every balance change, every new weapon/relic, every formation tweak touches them). They have zero cross-dependencies: each is a plain object or array of objects. Extracting them to a `data.js` module makes balance tuning a one-file review, enables diffing data changes separately from logic changes, and is zero-risk (the data has no side effects). This is the first step of the monolith split recommended in the first review (#10) and is independently valuable even if the rest of the split is deferred.

**How:** Create `data.js` with `export const WEAPON_STATS = { ... }` for each table. In index.html, replace the inline definitions with `import { WEAPON_STATS, ... } from './data.js'`. Change the `<script>` tag to `<script type="module">`. The harness already runs via puppeteer and supports modules.

**Target:** index.html shrinks by ~1,200 lines. data.js is independently reviewable. `node --check` and `npm test` pass.

**Impact/hour:** MEDIUM. ~2-3h for the extraction, no logic changes.

## Bugs and Risks

1. **Texture/geometry leak per run** (confirmed, worsened): 47 textures and 17 geometries per run cycle. `disposeEnemy` does not dispose `.map` textures from cloned materials. `_texCache` is never cleared. Will cause VRAM exhaustion on mobile after 3-4 runs. See Recommendation 1.

2. **Brennan wave-5 deaths (2 of 10 Kingsfield runs):** Not a bug, but a balance risk. A 20% chance of a player dying at wave 5 on normal difficulty with the "middle" character is too high. The wave-5 deaths happen when 4 consecutive level-ups offer no weapon card. See Recommendation 2.

3. **Map boundary black void** (run-parker.png): The camera can expose the void beyond the ground plane when the player is near the map edge. This is a camera clamp issue, not a world-generation issue. See Recommendation 6.

4. **Darkwood ford readability** (gpu-darkwood-4.png, fix2-darkwood-1.png): The knight and enemies are near-invisible at the ford crossing. The glow ring helps but the terrain and water are both dark. The fill-light raise was insufficient at this spot. See Recommendation 5.

5. **Quickblade evolution dmgMul 1.5 vs 2.0** (WEAPON_STATS.quickblade.evolution): Parker's evolved weapon deals 75% of the DPS of Dad's or Brennan's evolutions, all else equal. Not addressed since the first review despite being part of Recommendation 1. See Recommendation 8.

6. **Heap growth +61 MB per run** (profile-gpu-look.log: 1,009 -> 1,031 -> 1,092 MB): Beyond the texture/geometry leak, the heap grows by 61 MB per run. This suggests JS objects (enemy records, projectile arrays, VFX state) are not fully freed. The `clearRun` function zeroes arrays (e.g. `burnPatches.length = 0`, `state.enemies.length = 0`) but if closures or event listeners hold references, the GC cannot reclaim them.

7. **Scene children grow slightly** (1,202 baseline -> 1,210 after menu, 1,202 expected): 8 orphaned scene children per run. Likely point light or sprite objects not removed from the scene in `clearRun`.

8. **Profiler Brennan timeout** (profile-brennan-kingsfield.log): The Brennan profiler run timed out at 180s in the pre-look-pass run. Has not been re-run. This blocks profiling Brennan's specific performance characteristics (spear thrust VFX cost, shield orbit cost).

9. **No decal style consistency enforcement**: Some decals (cobble patches, bare soil) have a slightly photorealistic look (critic-look report) while buildings are Warcraft-style painterly. The retint pass (`retint-glb.mjs`) regraded GLB meshes but did not touch decal PNGs. A subtle style mismatch persists at close zoom.

10. **Speed goblin spawn rate ambiguity**: Speed goblins are spawned as a 15% chance on non-elite goblins from wave 4+ (line ~3313). This means speed goblin frequency is tied to goblin spawn rate, not independently tuned. On Darkwood (goblin weight 0.8x), speed goblins are rarer. On a hypothetical map with high goblin weight, they could be common enough to overwhelm.

## What I Could Not Assess

- **Ogre Levels 1, 3-5 balance**: No bot data exists for these levels. Ogre 0 and Ogre 2 are the only tested tiers.
- **Relic interaction edge cases**: The 9-relic pool has 84 possible 3-relic combinations. Only 3 combos have been observed in bot runs (phoenix/wolfsbane/windwalker, phoenix/greed/hunter, bloodletter/runes/windwalker per PLAN 12.5). Interactions like berserker + phoenix (does the revive at 35% HP immediately trigger berserker's below-50% bonus?) are untested.
- **Real-device GPU performance with the look pass**: The splat ground, 7,600 tufts, 12 decal instance meshes, 6 point lights with flicker, and the post shader (grade + vignette) have not been profiled on a real mid-range GPU. The harness runs with ANGLE/D3D11 or SwiftShader, which are not representative.
- **Stream ribbon at the ford**: The feathered ribbon code is in place (buildStreamRibbon with 0.35 * w feather width), but the ford crossing (where the stream meets the cobble track) may still have a visible edge. The stream3 screenshots show improvement but the ford area is only partially visible.
- **Shaman healing balance at Ogre 2+**: Shamans heal 12% every 4s. At Ogre 2+ enemy HP scaling, a shaman healing an ogre (60+ HP at wave 10) restores 7+ HP/tick. If the player's DPS at Ogre 2 is marginal, shamans could make ogre packs unkillable. No bot data at this intersection.
- **Audio latency and polyphony**: Procedural audio generates sounds per-play via Web Audio. With 40+ enemies dying per second at wave 15+, the polyphony and scheduling have not been measured for glitches.
- **Mobile / touch input**: Still no touch controls.
